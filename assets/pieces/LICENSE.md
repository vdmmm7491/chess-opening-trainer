as listed in lichess's COPYING.md, are:

The CC BY-NC-SA sets may only be used non-commercially, with attribution, and changes must be shared
under the same licence. This site is free and non-commercial. **If the site ever becomes commercial
(ads, paid features, sponsorships), delete the staunty, maestro and cardinal folders and their entries
in `js/theme.js`.** The default set is cburnett, which is GPLv2+ and compatible with this project's GPL-3.0.
