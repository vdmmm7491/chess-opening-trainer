# Opening Trainer – notes for AI assistants

- Pure static site: plain ES modules, **no build step, no package.json**. Deployed with GitHub Pages from `main` / root.
- External deps are loaded from pinned CDN URLs: chess.js `1.0.0` (jsDelivr `+esm`) and lichess cburnett piece SVGs. Stockfish is vendored in `engine/`: Stockfish 19 lite single-threaded WASM (`stockfish-19-lite-single.js` + `.wasm`, npm `stockfish` 19.0.1) is the engine; `stockfish.js` (Stockfish 10, asm.js) is only the fallback when WebAssembly is missing. Both load as same-origin Web Workers; GitHub Pages can't set COOP/COEP headers, so don't switch to a multi-threaded build. `Engine` marks itself busy as soon as a job starts (even before the worker is ready), so searches never overlap.
- Routing is hash-based in `js/app.js`. Each view in `js/views/` exports `render(root, {id, query})` and may return a cleanup function.
- `app.js` puts a "← Back" button (`.backbtn`) at the top of every page except the start page: it uses `history.back()` within the tab (depth kept in `history.state.n`), or goes up to `parentOf()` for a page opened directly. Views must append to `root`, not overwrite it.
- Famous games: scores in `js/games.js`, move-by-move commentary in `js/game-notes.js` (keyed by game id and ply). The viewer rates every move with `classifyMove` (in `js/chesscom.js`, `verify: true` re-checks apparent mistakes deeper) via `js/game-annotate.js`, saved in localStorage under `opening-trainer.famous-games.v1`; bump `VERSION` there when the method changes. Ratings are shipped pre-computed in `js/game-ratings.js` (regenerate with `tools/rate-games.html` after changing a game's moves or the rating method; entries whose moves differ are ignored and the browser rates the game itself). A note starting with an annotated move (`23...Nxg2!!`, `24.dxc7?`) overrides the engine's badge for that move.
- Game review: `#/analysis?review=w|b&moves=…` (built by `reviewHref` in `js/chesscom.js`) runs `engineCheck` on the whole game (both sides, `both: true`), then explains your worst move with `analyseMistake`. Once the review is ready the panel follows the board: stepping to any move shows its rating and the engine's best move (`showMove`), with "Best move" drawing it on the position before. Finished reviews are stored per game URL in localStorage (`opening-trainer.reviews.v3`, via `getReview`/`saveReview`); the Review tab's bulk check stores partial ones (your moves, first `CHECK_PLIES`). Move labels and thresholds live in `js/classify.js` (expected-score drops, not raw pawns); `legendHtml()` is the key shown next to badges. Between Top move and Solid sits **Precise** (2–5% expected-score drop); `refineKind()` re-splits top/solid in reviews and famous-game ratings saved before it existed. The review panel and the famous-games report show each side's accuracy (`gameAccuracy()`: mean of the arithmetic and harmonic means of per-move accuracies, book moves = 100). The analysis board's "Rate moves" button rates any move list.
- Progress page, from Chess.com games: "Since you started training" (`improvement()` in `js/improvement.js`, chart in `js/views/winrate-chart.js`) splits games at `startedLearning()` (first trainer session) and buckets them by week/2 weeks/month; "trained openings" = games following a practised line for 4 plies. Games come from `loadGames()` in `js/chesscom.js`, a cache shared with the Review tab.
- "What to learn next" (Progress page, `js/views/learn-next.js`) is `recommend()` in `js/recommend.js`: groups games by match-up (Black: White's 1st move; White: 1st move + Black's reply), keeps those scoring under 60% over 3+ games, and maps them to trainer openings by move prefix. It reads `OPENINGS` directly, so new openings/lines are picked up automatically.
- Only ONE shared engine instance (`getEngine()`). A new `analyse()` call stops the running search.
- `Board.setFen()` animates by diffing the old and new position (slides, check/mate/promotion effects; captured pieces just fade). Pass the real `lastMove` so promotions pair up; it can be switched off in Settings → Animations and respects prefers-reduced-motion.
- Progress lives in localStorage under `opening-trainer.v1` and is keyed by `openingId:lineIndex`. Never rename opening ids or reorder existing lines; add new lines at the end.
- Every opening needs `levels` (keys of `LEVELS` in `js/openings.js`) and a one-sentence `levelNote`.
- Every opening also needs a `tier` (key of `TIERS`: `first`, `next`, `master` = First Openings / Next Steps / Master Class). Tiers rate how hard the OPENING is and drive the slider on the home page, which shows one tier at a time (remembered in settings as `tier`, default from the level via `tierForLevel`). Tier colours are deliberately different from the `LEVELS` colours.
- Validate after editing `js/openings.js` by opening `tools/validate.html` (legal SAN, unique ids, valid levels, lines end on the trainee's move).
- Coach (trainer only): theory notes for every book move live in `js/line-notes.js` (Italian, Ruy, Scotch), `js/line-notes-d4.js` (QG, London, English, Vienna), `js/line-notes-black.js` and `js/line-notes-next.js` (Alapin, Advance, Catalan, Classical Sicilian, Accelerated Dragon, Dragon, Slav, QGD, Stonewall Dutch). Each is an array per line of `"SAN|note"` entries aligned with the line's moves (bare `"SAN"` = explained elsewhere). `js/notes.js`, `js/notes-routine.js` and `js/notes-routine-black.js` hold overriding notes keyed by the full SAN sequence (the routine files give fuller, position-specific reasons for castling, recaptures and so on). `coach.js` merges them into `THEORY`. When you add a line, add its notes too; the validator reports misaligned entries and moves without notes. The rule-based `explainMove` is only a fallback and is used to describe the engine's refutation moves.
- Licensing and design: the site's colours, board themes, move labels and badges are our own. Don't copy another chess site's palette, labels or icons. Only add piece sets or other assets whose licence allows it, and credit them in `README.md` (Credits & licence) and `assets/pieces/LICENSE.md`.
- Local test server: `powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File tools/serve.ps1` (port 8080).
- Punish mode (`#/punish/:id`, `js/views/punish.js`): common opponent mistakes per opening live in `js/punish.js` (`moves` up to and including the mistake, `punish` = your moves with their replies, starting and ending on your move, `elo` = the rating band (key of `BANDS`) where the mistake is typical). Mistakes must look natural for that band, not random piece hangs. Check every new entry with Stockfish (the punishment should be the engine's choice or close to it) and run `tools/validate.html`. Solved drills are stored under `punish` in progress.
