Lines vs. the Lichess Masters database

Checks every move of every line in js/openings.js against the Lichess Masters database (over-the-board games, 2200+), and shows how each line continues along the most-played master moves. Answers are cached in this browser, so the page can be reloaded and it resumes. It is slow on purpose (Lichess rate limits).
